## 🖐️ How to Jam & Act on Guitar

1. **Air Guitarist Mode ("Strum-to-Play")**:
   - Strum in the air with your right hand.
   - When you sweep your hand down (⬇️) or up (⬆️), **the vocal and guitar backing track plays along in sync**!
   - Every strum layers your live acoustic steel guitar chord plucks right over the track.
   - If you stop strumming, the song automatically pauses — so you drive the music!
2. **Interactive Fretboard (Left Hand)**:
   - Shows the exact finger pressing points (Frets 1–4, Strings 1–6) for all chords (`Em`, `D`, `C`, `G`, `Am`, `E`, `A`, `F`, `Dm`).
   - Color-coded finger markers:
     - 🔵 **1 (Index)**
     - 🟢 **2 (Middle)**
     - 🟡 **3 (Ring)**
     - 🟣 **4 (Pinky)**
3. **Volume Mixer**:
   - Adjust vocal/song volume and your hand strum volume independently.
4. **Load Any MP3**:
   - You can also upload any audio file from your computer and act/jam along to it live.
